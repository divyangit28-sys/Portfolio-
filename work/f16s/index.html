01 · Overview

Three problems, one platform

The first release takes a shipment from a new enquiry to finished documentation, in one place.

The problem 01
Manual paperwork
The same details retyped across every document.
What we built
AI document extraction
Fields are read from emails and documents, so people confirm instead of retyping.
The problem 02
No shipment visibility
Customers chase status by email, phone and WhatsApp.
What we built
Shipment tracking
Every shipment’s stage is visible on one board, without asking anyone.
The problem 03
No system for teams
Tasks handed out verbally and in chat.
What we built
Employee management
Each enquiry has an owner, and the team’s load is visible day by day.

06 · Solution

From an email to a ready job, without retyping

AI extraction does the data entry. People assign, check and confirm.

01
AI reads the enquiry email
Route, cargo, weight and size, pulled out on arrival.
02
Assign it and set the date
An owner opens the enquiry; the date makes it a job.
03
AI reads the documents
Invoice and packing list, read in seconds.
04
Review what it flagged
10 of 11 details filled, one marked to check.
Also in the first release
Shipment tracking
Every enquiry on one board by stage, so status is seen, not chased.
