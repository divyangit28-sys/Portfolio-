Interaction patterns
Create and edit forms
1Open the form in a drawer so the board stays in view.
2Pre-fill fields from extracted documents and mark which ones AI filled.
3Check each field on blur and keep the Save button visible.
4Save shows a toast. Cancel restores the last saved values.
Upload and validate documents
1Drop or browse. Each file shows its progress.
2AI extracts the fields and compares them across documents.
3Mismatches are flagged with both values side by side.
4A person confirms or corrects before the stage moves on.
Assign enquiries to staff
1Pick from the unassigned pool, oldest first.
2Choose an owner from a list sorted by workload.
3The owner is notified and the card moves to their column.
4Reassigning keeps the history in the message log.
Filter and bulk-update records
1Apply filter chips. The active filter count shows on the button.
2Select rows, or select all on the page with a link to select every match.
3A bulk bar offers Assign, Change stage and Export.
4Changes apply together, with Undo in the toast.
Confirm destructive actions
1The dialog names the record and what will be lost.
2Focus starts on the safe choice, Keep.
3Delete is red and labelled with the verb.
4Where possible, Undo is offered for 10 seconds instead.
Handle unsaved changes
1A dot on the title shows there are unsaved edits.
2Drafts are saved locally while typing.
3Leaving asks: Save, Discard or Keep editing.
4Nothing is discarded without a clear choice.
Display notifications and feedback
1Field errors appear inline, next to the field.
2Completed actions show a toast that clears after 4 seconds.
3Page-wide problems use a banner that stays until fixed.
4Things that happened while away collect under the bell with a count.
Accessibility and responsive rules
Contrast and readable type
Text 4.5:1 or better, and 3:1 for large text and control borders. Running text is 12 px or larger, and nothing goes below 11 px.
Keyboard and visible focus
Every action is reachable with Tab in reading order. The focus ring is always shown, Esc closes overlays, and focus returns to the control that opened them.
Clear labels and messages
Every field has a visible label. Errors say what is wrong and how to fix it, and icon buttons have spoken labels.
Click and touch targets
Every target is at least 44 × 44 px, with 8 px between neighbours. Icon-only buttons are 56 px in the product.
Responsive layouts
Follow the four breakpoints. The sidebar collapses, then becomes a drawer. Boards scroll sideways, and tables turn into cards below 768 px.
Status beyond colour
Every status carries a word and usually an icon: Verified, Mismatch, Missing, Over. Timelines also use icons and fill, not hue alone.
