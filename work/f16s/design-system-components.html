State and behaviour rules
StateHow it looksHow it behaves
DefaultToken colours at rest.Ready for input. The pointer becomes a hand on anything clickable.
HoverFill darkens one step, or an 8% navy overlay on light fills.Mouse only. Never the only way to reveal information.
Pressed12% overlay, or an inset shadow on filled buttons.Shown for the length of the press, then the action runs.
Focus2 px navy ring with a 2 px white gap, on every interactive element.Keyboard focus only. The order follows the reading order.
Disabled40% opacity, or the disabled grey for text and borders.No hover or press. A tooltip or helper line says why.
LoadingA spinner replaces the icon and the label changes to the verb in -ing form.The control locks to stop double submits. After 1 second, the content area shows a skeleton.
SuccessGreen check with a short confirmation.Inline next to the field, or a toast that clears after 4 seconds.
ErrorRed border with an icon and a message that says how to fix it.Input is kept. Focus moves to the first error on submit.
SelectedSelected-surface fill, plus a check or a bold weight.Announced to screen readers. Multi-select shows a count.
Expanded / collapsedThe chevron turns 180°. Content opens under its header.Header is a button with aria-expanded. One group opens at a time on boards.
ValidationHelper text sits under the field and becomes the error message in place.Fields check on blur, then on every change once an error has shown. Submit shows a summary at the top.
