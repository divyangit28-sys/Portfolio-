Discovery

The form looked like the problem. The wait was.

I mapped the flow and split a month of drop-off data by step. Most people left after doing all the work, while waiting for approval.

42%

of people who started a booking never finished it.

About 32,600 people a month. Cancellations were only 2.6%.

People

One flow, five groups who need it to work

Keep patients moving without weakening the checks everyone else relies on.

Prescription-first
bookingAI + HUMAN CHECK

Patients

Re-entered every test already on their prescription, guessed between overlapping benefits, then waited ~20 min unsure of approval.

Upload once, confirm, book while it's checked

Ops agents

Adjudicated every prescription and typed each detail in by hand.

AI pre-fills; agents review what's flagged

Insurers

Pay only for valid claims and worry about reused prescriptions.

Full validity check on every prescription; duplicates flagged

Employers

Want employees to use their benefits. Overlapping ones confused staff.

Best-value benefit applied automatically

The business

Losing ~32,600 bookings a month, plus costly manual entry.

More completed orders, lower cost per order

Approach

Start with the prescription. Use the wait.

The old flow made people describe their prescription, then upload it. The new one reads it first and lets them book during approval.

Before · form first

9 steps, one dead wait
Pick patient→Pick services→Pick tests→Pick benefit→Upload Rx→Agent types it in→Wait ~20 min→Come back→Pay

After · prescription first

AI reads, people decide
Upload Rx→AI reads it · 5–10 s→Confirm details→Book a preferred slot→Item-by-item result→Pay→Confirmed
In parallel

An agent approves each test while the patient picks a slot, so the ~7 minutes do useful work.

Working with tech and ops

Their answers reshaped the screens

I took my open questions to tech and ops before finalising. Each answer changed the design.

How long does parsing take?5–10 seconds

Changed: a live scan, so the wait feels like progress.

How confident is the AI?~90% printed, 50–70% handwritten

Changed: uncertain items turn amber for review.

What if parsing fails?One silent retry, then a manual route

Changed: the first failure stays invisible and nothing is lost.

Can a slot be held for 30 minutes?Not when demand is high

Changed: slots became preferences, confirmed after approval.

What happens with a duplicate?Warn, never block

Changed: a warning the patient can move past.

How long to build Phase 1?At least 1.5 months

Changed: the wait fix shipped first.
