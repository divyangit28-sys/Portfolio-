Every study starts with a question that is slightly wrong. The first week is spent finding out how. We sat with nurses at the end of a double shift and watched them write the same number three times. Nobody had asked them why, because the form had always worked on paper. A good interview feels like a conversation and is built like an experiment. The pause after an answer is where the real answer usually lives. Synthesis is slow on purpose: the sticky notes move until the pattern stops arguing back. Findings are only useful when someone with a roadmap can act on them by Monday. Write the uncomfortable slide first, then earn the right to show it. The Lead Researcher

About me

The person behind the goggles: what I care about, how I work, and why I'll always ask "why" one more time than you'd like.

DSpecimen: curious

I treat every product question as a hypothesis until real people prove it right or wrong.

I'm a UX designer and researcher with 4+ years in healthtech and B2B logistics. At Connect and Heal I started as a UX researcher and went on to work as an associate product manager across UX and product, until April 2026. Now I design for F16s, a platform for freight companies.

I trained in product design at NIFT, and I still like the part where the data surprises everyone, me included.

Methods
Interviews, usability testing, mixed methods, heuristic evaluation, A/B tests, funnel analysis
Design
Interaction design, information architecture, user flows, prototyping, conversational design, design systems
Based in
Bangalore, India
Operating belief
Taste is a hypothesis. Evidence is the test.
